The one fix that unlocks everything

Right now, spend can't be judged

Four things must connect for Google Ads to work. Three are ready; one is missing, and that single break stops the chain from telling the truth.

Ready

Storefront & feed

Products live and already listed on Google.

Missing

Analytics

Nothing watching how visitors behave.

Missing

Conversion & value

Sales and their £ value never reach Google.

Blocked

Smart bidding

Google can't optimise toward sales it can't see.

Good news: the fix is quick. Connect analytics and conversion value, and every pound becomes measurable, so Google can bid toward real sales instead of guesses. This is step one, not an afterthought.

The approach

How we'd test Google Ads properly

Not "switch it on and hope." A measured path that answers the real question, can Google Ads bring profitable sales, before scaling budget.

1

Make it measurable

Connect analytics and sales tracking with order value, so every click ties to a real outcome from day one.

→ understanding the data
2

Launch focused

Start where the fit is strongest: Shopping on the feed plus tight search around Islamic gifting and brand terms.

→ relevant traffic & visibility
3

Read the data

See which scents and searches actually buy, cut what wastes money, back what works, explained in plain language.

→ reducing wasted spend
4

Decide with evidence

Scale what's profitable, hold what's promising, drop what isn't, a call backed by results, not opinion.

→ profitable, scalable sales

The foundation is here. The lane is open. The next move is a measured start.

Crescent has the brand, the products and the demand. What's missing is the measurement to prove Google Ads works, and a focused start where it can. That's the direction I'd take it.
